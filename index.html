The Story

Conviction is a competitive advantage.

For twenty years I've backed the version of the future most people couldn't see yet. From a dorm-room prototype to companies serving a hundred million people, the through-line never changed: find the hard problem everyone avoids, assemble the team nobody else could, and out-stubborn the doubt.

  • 01

    Move before it's obvious

    The best opportunities look like mistakes right up until they look inevitable. I commit while the room is still hesitating.

  • 02

    Hire founders, not employees

    I build teams of people who would start their own thing — then give them a reason not to. Ownership compounds faster than talent alone.

  • 03

    Distribution is the product

    Genius unshipped is a hobby. Every venture is engineered around how it reaches the people it was built for.

  • 04

    Play the long game loudly

    Patience and ambition are not opposites. I think in decades and act with urgency every single day.
